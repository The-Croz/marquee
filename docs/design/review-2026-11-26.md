# App and web review (2026-11-26)

A walk through every iPhone screen (demo mode, iPhone 17 simulator, as Josh the admin) and the web app's Settings and tools (1440 wide), looking for bugs, missing pieces and design that could be better, with the web and the phone side by side.

## Fixed in this round

### Bugs

| Where | What was wrong | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Server, `POST /api/me/password` | Changing your password from the iPhone deleted every session, the phone's own included, and set a browser cookie the phone can't use. The phone was signed out by its own change. | A phone (bearer token) keeps its own session and everything else is signed out. A browser still gets a fresh cookie. Test: `appAuth.test.ts`. |
| iPhone, Downloads | Failed downloads (`needsALook`) were worked out but shown nowhere: they fell out of both chips. | A **Needs a look** chip appears when there are some. |
| iPhone, notifications | A notification tapped while the You sheet was open went to its page *behind* the sheet. | Opening a link closes the sheet first. |
| Web, sign in | The sign-in guard remembered where you were going, but the Login page always went Home. Every deep link (a notification, a bookmark, the phone's "On the web" rows) lost its page. | After signing in you land where you were going (same-site paths only, query kept). |
| Web, Rename preview and "What's already on…" sheets | The sticky button bar sat 20 px above the sheet's bottom, so the list showed underneath it. | Pinned to the very bottom. |
| Web on a phone, Clean up | The sticky action bars hid under the fixed tab bar. | They sit just above it. |
| iPhone, Manage | Sources said "0 working" in grey when there were none; Health said "None yet" in orange. | Both say "None yet", in orange. |

### iPhone design

- **Settings (the You sheet)** rebuilt like the web's Settings page and iOS's own: tinted icons, groups **You** (Account, Notifications, Which messages, Lock Screen cards, Discover), **Yours** (My requests & history, Calendar), **Manage** inline with live status (Health "1 problem", Sources "None yet", People "3", Library care "2 need a match"), **On the web** (every other Settings page, each opening its own page in Safari), **This iPhone** (server, app and server version, Sign out). The extra "Health, Sources, People and more" hop is gone.
- New screens for parity with the web: **Account** (name, password, Plex and single sign-on status, your phones with Remove) and **Discover** (which services get chips, trailers on the web).
- **Home and title pages**: content no longer slides under the clock and glass buttons with nothing behind it; a dark fade comes in as you scroll past the artwork.
- **Title pages** show ratings, who directed or made it, the trailer and the cast, like the web's title page (they were only on Discover's sheet).
- **Downloads**: the status card lines up with the title; chips are On the way, Needs a look, Sharing and **History** (the last copies sent, brought in or replaced).
- **Library care**: Scan for new files (with progress), rows that look tappable (Match ›), and links to Clean up, Put files in your library and Not watched on the web.
- **Health**: each problem has **Fix on the web** (or Set up), and "It said: …" where Marquee has the message.
- **Sources** and **People** have a row to add on the web instead of a sentence.
- **Turn down** has the web's three quick reasons as chips.
- **Requests**: "Recent" says what goes there when it's empty; the hint matches the buttons.

### Web design

- **Sidebar**: Calendar under Activity (it was only in the ⌘K bar), and **Library care** under Manage with a count of folders needing a match.
- **Library care** (`/library/care`): one page for every library tool, each with how it's doing (last scan with Scan now, folders needing a match, titles left out, import, rename movies or shows, clean up, not watched). The tool pages lead back to it. The Library's ⋯ menu is down to scan, needs a match, choose several, rename, and a link here.
- **Settings**: two columns on wide screens (everything fits without scrolling), and each row shows how it's doing from the Health check (Sources "None yet", Downloader "Working", Services "2 working", Health "1 needs you"). The side list puts an orange dot beside any page with a problem.

## Found, not done yet (suggested next)

1. **Demo mode can't show a live download.** Every fake site returns Dune copies, and Dune is already in the library, so Downloads, History and Live Activities are always empty in the demo. A second fake title with copies would let the phone's Downloads be checked by hand.
2. **Single sign-on still lands on Home.** The OIDC callback is a server redirect; it should carry the page you were going to, like password sign-in now does.
3. **iPhone: Never pick, and more History.** History shows the first page only (the web pages through it and filters); Never pick (copies turned down) is web only.
4. **iPhone: person pages.** Cast members are tappable on the web (their other titles); on the phone they aren't.
5. **Admins on Discover's sheet see "Ask for it" and "Add to your library…".** Both work, but for an admin they do nearly the same thing. One "Add" with the options (look for it now, which episodes, just add) would be clearer.
6. **Widgets and Shortcuts** (already in the plan's "Later"): On the way, Watching now; "Ask Marquee for…".
7. **Search opening with the keyboard up.** Tried and left out: with the field focused, iOS swaps the sheet's Done for its own search Cancel, so leaving Search took two taps. Worth another go with a custom field.
8. **Large text and haptics** (already listed as follow-ups): a pass at the largest Dynamic Type size, and haptics on Approve, Ask and Undo.
